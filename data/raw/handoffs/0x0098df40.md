## The entry-colour diagnosis in FOR_OTHER_AGENT was a misdirection
The zero carrier was NOT the cause. `edi` got the zero and `ebp` got `__imp__TlsGetValue` by itself once
the source shape was right. The three real causes, in the order they mattered:

1. **Four span-init stores instead of three.** ORIG's init is `start=0; count=0; capacity=0x80000000;`
   (three stores, exactly like sibling `FUN_00d0e4b0`). v2 also wrote `original=0`.
2. **One shared `char* buf` across the four allocations.** With a shared temp the allocator spilled it
   (a slot at 0x1c) and gave `ebx` to the **`0x80000000` constant**, which pushed `context` out of `ebx`
   into memory - that single decision is most of the 593. Assign each allocation's destination directly
   in both branches (ORIG 0x11d/0x132 for bounds, 0x15c/0x16e for rows). Then `ebx = context` at 0x050
   and 0x80000000 stays an immediate, exactly as ORIG.
3. **`volatile` in the marker guard.** ORIG has one `cmp byte ptr [ebx],0`; the volatile read forces
   `mov dl,[ebx]; test dl,dl` and costs an instruction.

## TIE 8 is allocator-inserted, not source-controllable
The loop body (0x196-0x22b) uses **ebp, ebx and esi as scratch**: `mov ebp,[esi]` 0x1d6,
`movzx ebx,[esi+0xe]` 0x1cf, `mov esi,[eax]` 0x19a. So at loop exit the allocator re-materialises all
three: `mov esi,[esp+0x18]` (rows), `mov ebx,[esp+0x1c]` (bounds), `mov ebp,[__imp__TlsGetValue]`.
Those are spill-restore / rematerialisation insertions; ORIG puts the (rematerialisable) import-pointer
load **last**, ours **first**. No source statement exists between the loop and `Trace(&DAT_010cc5fc)`,
so there is nothing to reorder; treat this as the *symptom* of the same one-slot rotation, not a knob.
