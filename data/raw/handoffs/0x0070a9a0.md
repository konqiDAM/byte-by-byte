Measured facts (these are the useful results):
1. **Every statement-form spelling loses the copy and one store**: `originalObject = fallback;
   object = fallback;` yields **extent 544, ndiff 35** — the compiler *merges* the two arm stores into
   one at the join (`mov [esp+0x14],esi` placed just before the `GetAttachment()` vcall) even with
   `volatile` on the local. Same for `object = fallback; originalObject = fallback;` and for the
   `const keep` alias form. So "just split the assignment" is **not** the fix.
2. `_ReadWriteBarrier()` in arm2 (6 placements tried, before/between/after the two statements) does not
   stop that merge (`ndiff 35/54/58`).
3. Taking the address (`*(SceneNode**)&originalObject = fallback;`) or a `volatile*` slot pointer only
   adds diffs (54/58).
4. `object = (SceneNode*)originalObject;` / `(SceneNode*)(originalObject = fallback)` are byte-equal to
   the draft — the reload is exactly what the volatile read produces, hence still ndiff 4.
5. Arm1 spellings (`originalObject = object;` vs `originalObject = limbContext.object;` vs a `t` temp)
   do not change the count.
