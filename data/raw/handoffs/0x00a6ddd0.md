## 5. The three named residues (all that is left)

ORIG is `fld <dst>; fadd <src>` at the two `pivotA` sites in R2 but `fld <src>; fadd <dst>` at the `r` sites
in R3/R4. One `add4` body cannot produce both, so one of the two call sites is written with a different
expression shape in the original. Everything else in R2 is identical. Fixing this makes R2 exact.

**Residue 2 — ORIG keeps the `setMul4` products on the x87 stack (the 16-byte extent gap).**
Where ORIG emits the pipelined 6-instruction form
`fld a; fld b; fld st(0); faddp st(2); fxch st(1); fstp dst` (two independent chains in flight), ours spills
and emits `fld; fadd; fstp` per component. 5 instructions × ~4 sites ≈ the 1650-vs-1666 gap. This is the
largest remaining item and it is pure x87 scheduling; the usual lever in this project is one more redundant
or missing live value, not a respelling.

**Residue 3 — ours hoists the `displayLine` argument pushes earlier than ORIG.**
ORIG does `push ecx` (id), then the last `fstp` of the vector, then `push -1; lea; push; push`; ours pushes
`-1`, `lea`, `push` before finishing the vector's `.w`.
