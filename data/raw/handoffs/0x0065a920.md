## What worked

1. **`AnimMap::Get` must intern a `FixedString` per lookup.** Taking `const char*` deleted all five `0xFUN_008242a0`
   calls and the handle dtors: extent 1481 → 1198. `Animation* Get(const FixedString& key) { Animation* value;
   return FUN_00bea7b0(key, value) ? value : 0; }` with call sites `map.Get(FixedString(DAT_00de2fa4))` restores
   the intern + `neg al; sbb eax,eax; and eax,[value]` + the dtor, and the five EH states 1..5.
2. **The pick block uses the member, not a local.** ORIG stores the manager into `this+0xa0` and *reloads* it for
   every lookup (`mov eax,[ebp+0xa0]; lea ecx,[eax+0x50]`). Writing `if ((m_a0 = (Sequence*)pick->FUN_00825200(…))
   != 0) { … m_a0->map.Get(…) … }` removed a 12-byte layout shift; without it every slot was 12 too high.
3. **`Node`/`NiPointer` from the pack header** (`NiPointer<T>::operator=(T*)`) reproduce the whole refcount dance
   byte for byte; `Node` needs `Attach(Node*,bool)` at vtable slot 55 (`+0xdc`), `refs` at +0x04, `controller` at
   +0x20, `rotation` at +0x34, `position` at +0x58, `sizeof == 0xac` (the `push 0xac` in the ORIG).
4. **`FixedString` by *value* as the `Get` parameter drops the frame to 0x94 and makes R1 byte-exact** — but MSVC
   then refuses to inline `Get` (a real `call` appears, extent 1408). Recorded as a lever, not a fix.

## The frame gap, measured exactly (2026-11-14 00:10Z, ~10 variants)

The `0x94` reached by the by-value probe is **not** caused by the by-value parameter; it is caused by `Get`
*not being inlined*. Measured:

| shape | frame | insns | extent | ndiff | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Get(const FixedString&)` inlined (base) | 0x98 | 432 | 1476 | 154 | 4 temp slots |
| `Get(FixedString)` **not** inlined | **0x94** | — | 1408 | 150 | R1 exact, but a real `call` replaces the inlined ternary |
| `Get(FixedString)` + `__forceinline` | 0x98 | 483 | 1663 | 227 | **it does inline** — and then the by-value parameter costs a copy (+1 ctor/dtor per site, +51 instructions) |
| `Get(const FixedString)` + `__forceinline` | 0x98 | 483 | 1663 | 227 | same |
| one inline builder with a `bool xAxis` flag | 0x98 | 432 | 1476 | 154 | byte-identical to the base, so the two cos/sin pairs already merge |
| angle hoisted into `float ang` | 0x98 | — | 1466 | 177 | loses the store/reload |
| braces around the three pick lookups | 0x98 | — | 1505 | 180 | worse |

So `__forceinline` does override the heuristic (483 instructions vs the inlined base's 432) — the wall is the
**by-value parameter's copy**, not the inliner. And the surplus 4 bytes are exactly one map-value temp slot:

- base temps: `E-164` (value ×4 **and** the sincos angle), `E-160` (the 5th value), `E-156` (cos/sin), `E-152` (cos/sin)
- ORIG temps: `0x14` (value **and** angle), `0x18` (handle **and** cos), `0x1c` (sin) — three slots

The 5th lookup (`Left`) is the one that gets its own slot instead of sharing. Nothing tried makes it share
(explicit shared `Animation* value` at function scope: still 0x98; braces; reordering).

**Also learned:** the by-value parameter *does* put `x`/`z` exactly where ORIG has them (`-120`/`-84`) — the
matrix slots are a consequence of the same allocation decision, so fixing the 5th temp should fix both.

## Hypotheses / next steps

1. **Frame 0x98 → 0x94 (the gate; R1 goes exact the moment it lands).** Now known precisely: the base needs FOUR
   4-byte temps (`E-164` value+angle, `E-160` the fifth map value, `E-156` cos/sin, `E-152` cos/sin) where ORIG
   needs THREE (`0x14` value+angle, `0x18` handle+cos, `0x1c` sin). The surplus is that the **fifth** lookup
   (`Left`, `m_ac`) gets its own value slot instead of sharing `E-164`. No by-value/reference/forceinline/temp
   reshuffle fixed it: any *inlined* `Get` costs 4 bytes, and `__forceinline` on the by-value form inlines fine
   (483 insns) but pays a FixedString copy per site. Untried: give the fifth lookup a shorter-lived value
   (e.g. write the `Left` lookup after `FUN_0045c7f0(0.0f)` — but ORIG has it before, so only as a probe), or
   find a `Get` body whose out-parameter is a *single* temp for all five sites without a declared local.
2. **x/z slot order** — `x`/`z` are at `E-84`/`E-120` in the base and at `E-120`/`E-84` (ORIG's positions) as
   soon as the by-value `Get` shape is used. They move with the same allocation decision as (1), so fix (1) first.
3. **Tail (R6)**: ORIG compares the member (`cmp dword [eax+0x20],0; je; mov ecx,[eax+0x20]; jmp; mov ecx,offset
   DAT_0108edc8`) with the node in `eax` and the controller in `ecx`; ours loads the member into `eax` and tests
   it. The `NiPointer` assign of `DAT_01076b04` also picks `edi` where ORIG uses `ebx`. Both look like knock-ons of
   the 4-byte frame pressure: fix (1) first and re-measure before touching the tail.
4. **Pick block scheduling**: ORIG emits `push 0xde2fa4` *before* `mov [ebp+0xa0],eax`; ours the other way round —
   again a scheduling knock-on.
