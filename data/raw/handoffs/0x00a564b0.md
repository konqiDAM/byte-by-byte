## What worked (the fix)

Expand **only** the `n` cross into three explicit member assignments; keep the `m` cross as the inline
helper call:

```cpp
    Vec4 n;
    n.x = e1.y * e2.z - e1.z * e2.y;
    n.y = e1.z * e2.x - e1.x * e2.z;
    n.z = e1.x * e2.y - e1.y * e2.x;
```
instead of `n.SetCross(e1, e2);`. Byte-identical to ORIG except the one address reloc
(`fcomp dword ptr [0xeaad58]`). `(a.y * b.z) - (a.z * b.y)` with parens is also identical (same 3-byte
reloc-only diff), so the parens are not the lever — the **statement form** is.
