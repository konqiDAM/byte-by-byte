## What worked (in order of value)

1. **`radiusA` as a `Scratch` member declared between `shapeEnd` and `outcome`.** VC8 allocates a struct's
   members in *reverse* declaration order, downward from `0x28`, so a member placed immediately before `outcome`
   lands on `0x24`. This took `--slots` from "28 in place, 1 moved" to **29/0** and ndiff 17 → 15.
2. **The loop-head monitor pointer stored in `scratch.outcome`** (the twin's `scratch.outcome.monitor` form,
   with a reload for `setEnd`). Before, the compiler invented a fresh spill slot at `0x14`, which then collided
   with `radiusA`. ndiff 15 → **13**, `diffs` → 2.
3. `_ReadWriteBarrier()` between each monitor's name store and its `rdtsc` (otherwise `rdtsc` is hoisted above
   the string stores — 6 instructions).
4. Typed members instead of `char[]` and explicit `allocateStack`/`deallocateStack` instead of `LocalBuffer`'s
   destructor (killed the GS cookie and the SEH frame: 1227 → 1088 bytes).
5. Two distinct cache types: agent `Cache {int first, second; UInt32 state;}` (0xc, align 4) vs output
   `CacheState` (`time@0x20`, `Basis transform@0x40`, 0x70, align 16 — hence `Temporary.cache` at `+0x1010`).
6. `StateRecord* state = &agent->m_state;` for the zeroing guard; `int` (not `float`) for `unk50/unk54`.
