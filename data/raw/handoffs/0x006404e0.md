## What it is

A Sudoku-like solver: `unsigned Solver::FUN_006404e0(Grid& grid)` counts the distinct values in each
row (helper `FUN_0063fcd0`), takes the minimum, and recurses on a sub-grid.

## The two independent differences

1. **the slot swap** (the headline): the original keeps the dword `q` at esp+0x14 and the byte
   `lowest` at esp+0x18; every candidate has `lowest` at +0x14 and `q` at +0x18.
2. **the extent gap (2 bytes)**: the compiler materialises `&grid.rows[r]` as
   `lea ecx,[ecx+ebx*8]` because that address is used by both the count load and the data load;
   the original keeps `grid.rows` in ecx and folds `ebx*8` into **both** operands (2 bytes shorter).
   Inlining the member access fixes the *count* load (`mov esi,[ecx+ebx*8+4]`, still 445 bytes) but
   the data load is still hoisted as a `lea`.
