## The ONE blocking mechanism (address CSE + spill)

VC2005's GVN materializes reused disp-4/8 component addresses into registers and spills them across the `__CIsqrt` call so the later reuse (the cb subtraction re-reads b and c) can use them. ORIG has **zero** such materializations: every component address is a direct SIB operand everywhere (`fsub [esi+ebx+4]` in BOTH the ab and cb subtractions). Confirmed by micro-test (temp\micro1.cpp): given a reused disp-4 address VC materializes it (`lea esi,[ecx+eax+4]`) — into callee-saved regs there. In the big function my regs are busy so it spills to +0x10/+0xa0/+0xa4/+0xac (4 extra slots that ORIG uses for other things).

My residue from this: 
- `lea eax,[esi+ebx+4]` hoisted into the ab-multiply dance, spilled to a slot, `fsub [eax]` for ab.y; same for ab.z, ac.y, ac.z (and the cb reads consume the spills via `fld [edx]`/`fld [eax]`).
- ORIG recomputes everything as direct operands.

Tried and failed to kill it: direct expressions (draft 5), array-member spelling (draft 4). The two uses (ab.y and cb.y of data[j]) are textually identical in ORIG's source too, so whatever makes ORIG's compiler not CSE them is still unknown. Next ideas below.
