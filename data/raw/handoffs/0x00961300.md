   candidate flag set: `/Od /Oy-` emits the **ebx frame** (`push ebx / mov ebx,esp / push ecx /

   sequence. (It is `/Oy`, frame-pointer *omitted*, despite the pack saying `/Oy-`.)

## What still differs (cand4.cpp, under `/O2 /Oy`)

- Frame `sub esp,0x54` vs ORIG `0x74` — 0x20 short. ORIG has 16 distinct scalar slots
  (`0x10 i`, `0x14 s/k`, `0x18 this`, `0x1c count`, `0x20-0x2c px..pw`, `0x30-0x3c 2*px..2*pw`,
  `0x40-0x48 t1,t2,t3`) **plus** `Q`@0x50 and the 0x20 object@0x60. Our compiler coalesces
  `d0..d3`/`t1..t3` into fewer slots, i.e. the source does not keep them simultaneously live the way
  ORIG's does. Candidate mechanism: ORIG's `2*pi` terms are the four separate statements
  `float d0 = px+px; ...` *before* the `Q[i] = edi->w[i] + di` statements, with `px..pw` still live
  across them (they are read again at ORIG 0x100ish); try making `px..pw` live past the doubling
  (e.g. `Q[i] = w[i] + (px+px)` written as separate `float` locals assigned from the *doubled* temps,
  or add a second consumer of `px` after the doubling).
- `esi`/`edi` are swapped: ORIG `esi = p1+8`, `edi = p2`; ours has `edi = p1+8`, `esi = p2`.
  Declaring `P2* edi` first did not move it (proved); try the reverse (already `cand.cpp` order) with

- `this`/`count` are spilled at `[esp+0x28]`/`[esp+0x2c]` instead of ORIG's `0x18`/`0x1c` — follows
  from the frame being too small; fix the slot count first.
- After that the residue is x87 rank/order inside the P-dot and cross blocks — treat those as the
