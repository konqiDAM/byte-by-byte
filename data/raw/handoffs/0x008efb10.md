**Class: stack_layout (frame shape).** ORIG's frame is esp-based and **16-byte aligned**, i.e. there is a
`__declspec(align(16))` object at **function scope**; ours is the plain ebp frame. Measured: putting
`align(16)` on the try-scoped `Desc` gives VC8's *alloca* form
(`push ebx; mov ebx,esp; sub esp,8; and esp,-16; add esp,4; mov ebp,[ebx+4] …`) — extent 705, still
wrong prologue. So the aligned object must be declared at function scope (outside the `try`).

Second consequence of the same cause: frame `0x78` vs `0x24`+`0x40`; the missing ~0x38 bytes are that
aligned local plus the slots it pushes.
