   ORIG's inlined x87 at `+0x244..+0x37f` is *exactly* that body (scale at `[esp+0x20]`, `ret` at `[esp+0x24]`,

   instead of `setCross(quat.getImag(), direction)`; everything else (positive `qreal`, positive dot,
   `fmul qword [__real@…]` for `w*0.0`) is identical. Written as a `__forceinline` free function.
3. Three mechanical fixes took 256 → 309 of 447 instructions:

## Hypotheses / next steps

1. **The 16 missing bytes are a value that is LIVE ACROSS a region, not merely declared.** The 0x54 and 0xd4
   holes are 16-aligned and unreferenced, i.e. slots the allocator reserved and the store/load elimination later
   emptied. A caller `cross` out-param is *not* it (measured: frame stays 0x118). Try, in this order:
   (a) a `hkVector4` that is alive from R4 to the end of R5 (e.g. an intermediate kept in the source);
   (b) `visit`-style: something allocated before `matrix` and freed right after it.
   Diagnose by watching `sub esp` and whether `matrix` (0xe4) and `inverse` (0xd4) move **together** by 0x10.
2. **Then the rotation/converted swap** (0x94 vs 0xb4): `rotation` is defined in R4 (`+0x17a`), `converted` in R5
   (`+0x383`), and ORIG gives the *earlier* value the *lower* address. Look for what made ORIG's allocator free
   0xb4 before `converted` needed it.
3. Then R7 (4 lines: `this`/visit slots only), R6 (11: the else path, `rep movsd` M3 copy + V3 copy + flags —
   structure already matches), R3/R4 (16 each, slot operands only).
