## The mechanism, established rather than guessed
* The **vcall template at this site always yields vptr→edx, fp→eax** (21 receiver spellings
  tested: `(*ctx)`, `ctx[0]`, `(&ctx[0])`, `((Ctx*)ctx)`, `((Ctx&)*ctx)`, `ctx->Ctx::v1e4()`,
  named `Ctx*`, `Ctx&`, `const`, `Info* info = ...` — all byte-identical).
* The **manual load reuses eax for the destination whenever the base is eax** (MSVC's
  load-into-base optimisation), and does *not* reuse when the base is edx. So `eax` (vptr) +
  `edx` (fp) requires the vptr to be **live past the load**.
* The pick parity (eax↔edx; ecx is reserved for the receiver) is the only knob that has ever
  moved a byte in this function, and the head's **conversion** is what sets it.
