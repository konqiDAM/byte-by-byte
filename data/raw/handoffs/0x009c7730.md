## Conclusion / next ideas

The residue is **not** a source shape: 20+ spellings that keep the 684-byte instruction stream
byte-identical never move a slot, and every spelling that *does* move a slot also changes the
instructions. The four slots are chosen by the /O2 local-coalescing pass, and the choice runs
mirrored against our symbol numbering. That is the "compiler tie-break" class in
`handoffs\lessons-2026-11-12.md` §12 - the build host's longer solver is the right tool.

If someone tries again, the two untested levers are:

1. make the *four* slot symbols be created in the target's order by a construct that does not
   change the emitted code, e.g. let `_setTransformedPos` take `v0,v1,v2` from the caller
   (parameters are numbered before locals) - note the caller-visible frame must stay 0xd4;
2. `#pragma`-free re-scoping of the three `v`s into a nested block *inside* the loop body so the
   symbol ids (not the code) change order.

Both are guesses; the mirrored-pair symmetry (`swap 0x10/0x14` + `swap 0x18/0x1c`) says one
ordering flip in the front end fixes all 18 instructions at once.
