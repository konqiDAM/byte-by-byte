## What the function is
Havok-style constraint/chain solver, two passes over `numLinks` (u16 at
`chain+2`) elements. Layout after the 0x10-byte chain header:
`Jac jacs[n]` (48 B each = 3 aligned vec4: lin, angA, angB),
`Diag diags[n]` (12 B: lower/diag/upper), `float lambdas[n+1]`,
`int offsets[n+1]`. Forward pass computes per-link Jacobians via
`calcJv`-style vec4 cross/dot products and solves a tridiagonal system
(`lam[i+1] = (rhs - lam[i]*lower)/diag`); backward pass back-substitutes
(`x = lam[i+1] - x*upper`), accumulates impulses, and applies
invMasses/jac-weighted velocity updates to two Accum records per link.
