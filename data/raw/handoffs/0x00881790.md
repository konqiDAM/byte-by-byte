## What did not work (the wall)

The target needs **both** facts at once:
1. head store `DAT_0106e588 = windowed` **before** the item clear (source order `D i n p`), and
2. the shared zero constant living in **ecx** (it is also the 2nd argument pushed at 0198).

They are mutually exclusive in every shape I could write:

| source order | zero register | ndiff |
|---|---|---|
| `n D i p`, `i n D p`, `i n D p`-like | **ecx** | 2 (store in the wrong place) |
| `D i n p` (target order) | **eax** | 28 (whole tail rotates) |

Mechanism: the zero's first use is the item clear. With the head store first, `eax` is already
dead when the item clear happens, so the allocator materialises the constant in `eax`; the
post-pass scheduler then cannot hoist `xor eax,eax` above the store (it would clobber the value
being stored), so the whole trailing register assignment (`state` load takes `ecx`, `push eax`
instead of `push ecx`) follows. In the 2-diff builds the zero's first use precedes the store,
`eax` still holds the loaded next-pointer, the constant goes to `ecx`, the xor hoists to 0x17a —
but then the store is 5 bytes too late.

Variants tried (~35, all byte-identical to one of the two builds above):
`windowed` named local vs none, `const ListNode* const`, `ListNode* const`, `const long zero = 0`
declared before/after the read and used for the clears and/or the call argument, `0L`, `NULL`,
typed `(ListNode*)0`/`(ModeItem404*)0`, `__forceinline` `ResetNode(ListNode*)` helper,
`__forceinline` `SetPoolHead()` setter, comma expression, alias local `ListNode* pn = poolNode`,
keeping `windowed` live past the clears, `f3check --permute` (no improvement, score 30).
