## Read on the allocation, for whoever continues

Evidence says VC8 SP2/SP3 slot reuse here is **scope-driven, not live-range-driven**:

* `base`: the `rotate` local lives in a *nested block* → it is laid out **last** among the
  36-byte locals and gets the top slot 0xe0, then shares that slot with the later
  `FUN_00424020` sret temp.
* `original`: `rotate` sits at 0x98 (immediately above `inverse` at 0x74) and its slot is
  later reused by `spin`. So in the real source `rotate` must be laid out *between*
  `inverse` and `frame`, i.e. it must be in the **same scope as `inverse`** while still
  giving up its slot to `spin`.
* But every spelling that hoists `rotate` into the if-scope makes it share nothing
  (`0xe0` + its own sret slot at 0x108) → frame 0x118.
* And `localPosition` (declared before `position`/`direction`) gets the *higher* slot
  0x40, while `axisV` (declared far later) gets 0x34 ⇒ the layout pass is **not** in
  declaration order either. `localPosition` in the original sits at 0x34 = the slot mine
  gives `axisV`.

Next things worth trying (in rough order of promise):

1. A probe TU (see `exp91\probe*.cpp`, `ax_probe_home.*`) that reproduces just
   "one 36B matrix in a nested block + one 36B matrix later" to find the spelling that
   makes the nested-block matrix take the slot directly above `inverse` *and* keep the
   frame. If `rotate` can be made to land at 0x98, the center/Point2-if/invHeight
   deltas and (probably) the 0x04f1 operand order may all fall out — that was the
   observed coupling in the 27-diff variant.
2. Then attack `localPosition` 0x40→0x34 / `axisV` 0x34→0x40 (they must *swap*), which
   the same 36B-scope fix did not move.
3. The `FUN_004752c0` sret temp should land on `center` (0x5c) rather than `axisV`
   (0x34) — likely fixed for free once (1)+(2) are right, since it is a
   "which dead slot is picked" consequence.
