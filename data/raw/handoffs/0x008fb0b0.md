## The `cdq` pair — SOLVED
`mov eax,[eax] / cdq / mov eax,[eax+8] / or [eax+0x30],1 / mov [esp+0x18],edx` is
`v = r ? (__int64)...->object : 0;` with `__int64 v` and only the LOW half used afterwards (`(Obj*)(int)v`). The low
half is forwarded in `eax` and never stored; the high half is stored. Nothing else tried produced it. Keep this.

## What still differs (classes)
1. **`r ? call : 0` null test is deleted by the compiler** (8 bytes: `test ecx,ecx / je / jmp / xor eax,eax`).
   ORIG keeps it *after* `cmp dword ptr [ecx+8],0`, so ORIG's source dereferences a **different load node** than the
   one it then tests. Proven with `p2.cpp`: `if (data[i]->f8 != 0) continue; N* r = data[i]; v = r ? ... : 0;`
   **keeps** the test (`mov ecx,[..]; cmp [ecx+8],0; jne; test ecx,ecx; je`), while `if (r->f8 ...)` with
   `r = data[i]` folds it away. Reproducing this shape in the real function (`r1.cpp`, `r2.cpp`, `s1.cpp`, `s3.cpp`)
   does keep the test but makes MSVC 2005 materialise a **zero constant in `edi`** (`xor edi,edi; cmp esi,edi` for
   `if (!node)`), which adds a third callee-saved register, grows the frame by 4 and moves every slot
   (55 diffs, extent 264). `s2.cpp` (pointer `o` instead of `__int64 v`) avoids that at 35 diffs / extent 266 —
   **this is the most promising line: get the fresh-deref shape without the zero-in-edi constant.**
2. **`cur` colouring:** ORIG `edi`; the clean body gives `ebx` (`v2`), `z1` gives `edi`. The data temp is `edx` in
   ORIG and in `z1`, `eax` in `v2`. The two go together — whichever change fixes the null test also has to keep
   `edi`/`edx`.
   **Latest finding (t1.cpp / t2.cpp, 35 diffs, extent 266):** the fresh-deref shape
   (`if (list.data[i]->unk08 != 0) continue; Node* r = list.data[i];` with an `Obj* o = r ? ... : 0` ternary) gives
   `cur` in **edi** *and* keeps the **null test**, with the frame at the right size (266). What is left there is only
   (a) the compare materialising the element (`mov ecx,[..]; cmp ecx,[edi+8]`) where ORIG does
   `mov eax,[edi+8]; cmp [edx+esi*4],eax`, (b) the data temp in `eax` instead of `edx`, and (c) the `cdq` (it needs
   the `__int64 v` form, which is what reintroduces the zero-in-edi problem in `s1`). **Start the next attempt at
   `t2.cpp`** — it is the closest complete shape.
3. **Loop alignment:** ORIG has the 2-byte `mov edi,edi` at `0x7e`; a 3-byte `lea ebx,[ebx]` appears when the code
   before the loop is a byte longer. Follows from class 1/2, not a separate problem.
4. **`call dword ptr [0xd9b080]`** shows as `unresolved original symbol: __imp__TlsGetValue@4` only while the extent
   differs — `__imp__TlsGetValue@4` is ambiguous in `f3decomp_symbols.json` (0xd9b080 and 0xd9b0a4), so f3check
   derives it from the original bytes at the same offset. It resolves by itself once the function length matches.
   Not a defect; do not flag.
