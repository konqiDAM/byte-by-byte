## What worked (the hard part)

Everything inside the inlined `hkVector4::setRotatedDir` + final subtract is x87 code
whose stack-slot order MSVC 2005 picks globally. Many source-level variants of
dot3/cross/addMul4/setMul4 changed NOTHING (compiler canonicalizes them); the decisive
change was at the END of the function:

- Manual `p.m_quad[i] -= v.m_quad[i];` x4 → x87 stack stayed rotated → dot3 emitted
  products in y,x,z order and every `fmul st(n)` operand was off.
- Replacing it with a named `hkVector4::sub4(const hkVector4&)` helper → scheduler
  flipped to the original's exact sequence: all 148 instructions match modulo
  relocation display (`[0xdb7de8]` vs `[0]`, `je 0x925b02` vs `je 0x192`, etc.).

Lesson for x87-heavy Havok functions: the register/stack allocator is global — a helper
ANYWHERE in the function (even the epilogue subtract) changes scheduling inside an
inlined math routine. Try restructuring distant code, not just the divergent region.

## What did not work

~30 variants of dot3/cross/addMul4/setMul4 expression order, operand order, temp
declarations, loop forms, arg swaps, `direction` temp copies — all produced identical
or worse diffs. The y,x,z product order was invariant until the sub4 change.
