### The law this establishes

**A free callee-saved register is always filled by an invariant pointer load, never by the
counter.** Suppress one hoist and the next pointer takes ebx (d2); suppress both and the reload
of the spilled pointer's own slot takes it (d4). Extending a value's live range past the loop
does not move the hoist and costs instructions (d6). Measured ranking:

    hoist(outNormal, 4 body uses)  >  hoist(outPos, 1 use)  >  filterInfo (2 cold uses)
      >  counter (2 latch uses, P = 4)

Combined with the earlier runs: keeping the **literal** constant 0 in a callee-saved register
across the raycast call needs the allocator to prefer register over rematerialisation for a
constant, and every source form that forces it (a real load: `collector.m_size`, `m_size >> 32`)
adds an instruction ORIG does not contain and repacks the frame to `sub esp,0x3c8`.
