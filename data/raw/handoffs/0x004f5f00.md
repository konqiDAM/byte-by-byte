### Why the two requirements have not coexisted

`z` (live 0x004d–0x0073) and `sound` (def 0x0062) compete for `edi`/`ebp`. An `extras` web that lives from the
entry block across the switch makes sound win `edi` (the original allocation); without it sound loses and the
pair swaps. But every structure that gets the merge `lea ebp` **before** `push ecx` is one that has no entry web.
So the lever is an entry-live `extras` web whose *merge* materialisation is load-like rather than a store.
