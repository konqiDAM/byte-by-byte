`__assume` and `__noop` are the only MSVC constructs *defined* as "use the expression, emit nothing", and
the front end consumes both before register allocation. Everything else is folded in the preprocessor or by
the optimiser. So the use counter counts materialised instructions only - the hypothesis the previous three
waves were built on is false.

**The byte budget also closes the door independently.** The ORIG's 105 bytes are real code plus a genuine
4-byte alignment filler at 0x2c (`8d 64 24 00`, the loop starts 16-aligned at 0x30). Any materialised 4th
use either overwrites the filler's four bytes or moves the extent to 109. There is no slack to hide one in.

**The residue is exactly this, and nothing else** (`cand.cpp`, f3check: `status=wip, ndiff_insn=2,
extent=105, errors=[]`, sha256 `DF810432EADD5737CDD8AF177C44A4E24EA0CCFDFD32670E4B5A9B590D20A3D0`):

```
ORIG  0x02c  8d 64 24 00   lea esp,[esp]            (alignment filler)
YOURS 0x02c  8b 54 24 0c   mov edx,[esp+0xc]        (reload of the item param, same 4 bytes)
```
