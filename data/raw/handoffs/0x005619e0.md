`mov eax,[ebp+0xc] / mov edx,[ebp] / … / lea ecx,[esp+0xa0]`, ours
`mov edx,[ebp+0xc] / mov eax,[ebp] / … / lea eax,[esp+0xa0]`. Everything before it (181
instructions) is identical, registers included.

Remaining residue, all of one kind:

1. **Tail merge (the extent gap).** ORIG *duplicates* the `~BSStringT` + `push dtor/4/0x18` +
   `lea` + `push` prefix at `if (!texture) return false;` (+0x4c8) and jumps into the shared
   state-3 tail at **+0x2fd**; ours jumps to the whole shared copy at **+0x2de**. That is the
   1451 → 1484 gap (30 bytes) plus 3 bytes of jump encoding, and it is why the two `jmp`s in R2
   differ.
2. **Five colouring sites** (instructions otherwise identical, eax/edx/ecx only): warning printf
   +0x28e; the `sex*3` index for `modelX` +0x2c9; the shared false tail's `lea` +0x2de; the
   FindTextureByPath block +0x357; the `__ehvec_dtor` `lea` at the !model6 (+0x415) and !texture
   (+0x4c8) sites.

They are **one bistability**: ORIG's two cleanup prefixes differ in the `lea` register, so VC8
could not merge them; ours are byte-identical, so it did — and merging changes liveness, which is
what re-colours the five sites. In ORIG the eight `lea reg,[esp+0x44]` sites take
eax (0x2de), edx (!texture), ecx (!model6), edx (findtex-true), eax/edx/eax (the three early
returns), ecx (return-true tail); ours take edx at both the shared block and the !texture site,
which is exactly what makes them mergeable.
