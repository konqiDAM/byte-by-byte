## The residue, classified: a compiler tie-break (register colouring), not a source shape

**114 of the 126 differences are one cyclic rotation of three callee-saved registers.**
Three values are live across the whole function and take ebx/esi/edi:

| value | target | draft |
|---|---|---|
| `this` | `edi` | `ebx` |
| the constant **20** (group of the empty table entries) | `esi` | `edi` |
| the constant **0** (null labels, `push 0` args, `== 0` tests) | `ebx` | `esi` |

`ebp` is the 4th callee-saved register and holds the race-morphs pointer in both
(`mov ebp,[ecx+0x110]` at 0x90c, dies at 0x94c) — so the whole push set is identical
(`push ebx; push ebp; push esi; push edi`) and everything else in the function is byte-identical.

Consequences of the rotation that look like shapes but are not:
* `0x091e cmp eax,ebx` (target) vs `test eax,eax` (ours) — the target compares against the
  materialised zero *register*; when the zero lives elsewhere the compiler folds it to `test`.
* `0x137c`/`0x139c cmp al,bl` vs `test al,al` — same.
* every `[edi+0x74]`, `[edi+0xd8]`, `[edi+eax*4+0x28]`, `push ebx`, `cmp ecx,ebx` line.

**One real stack-slot difference** (6 lines, R10/R11): the loop's fold temporaries sit at
`esp+0x18` and `index` at `esp+0x1c` in the target; ours has `index` at `esp+0x18` and the
temporaries at `esp+0x1c` (0x982, 0x9a0, 0x9a8, 0xa29, 0xa40, 0xa44, 0xa4b, 0xa62).
Note 0xa29: the target spills the slider pointer to `esp+0x18`, ours reuses the dead `value`
slot `esp+0x14`. Since the target's slot at 0x18 is used both for the clamp temporary and for
the slider pointer, the target very likely has a *function-scope* named local there (a
`float bound`) declared between `value` (0x14) and `index` (0x1c).

Cost-model check (compiler_tips "colour by cost"): the target's ranking cannot be
